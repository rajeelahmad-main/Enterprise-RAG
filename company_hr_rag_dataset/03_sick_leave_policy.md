# Sick Leave and Absence Reporting
Document ID: HR-003 | Owner: People Operations

Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per calendar year under this fictional policy. Notify your manager and the attendance channel before your normal start time whenever possible. If illness continues for more than three consecutive working days, People Operations may request reasonable supporting documentation consistent with applicable law.

Sick leave is not a substitute for annual leave. Do not share private medical details with coworkers; provide necessary documentation only through the approved confidential HR process. If an absence may be work-related, tell People Operations so the incident can be reviewed.
