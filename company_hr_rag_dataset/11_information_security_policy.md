# Information Security Policy
Document ID: IT-011 | Owner: IT Security

Employees must use unique passwords, multifactor authentication, approved password managers, and company-approved storage. Never share credentials or approve an unexpected login prompt. Lock the screen when leaving a device unattended.

Store company data only in approved systems and give access based on business need. Do not upload confidential files to personal cloud drives or unapproved AI tools. Verify unexpected payment or access requests through a separate trusted channel.

Report suspected phishing, malware, lost devices, accidental disclosure, or unusual account activity to security@northstar.example immediately. Do not delete evidence or attempt to investigate a suspected incident independently.
