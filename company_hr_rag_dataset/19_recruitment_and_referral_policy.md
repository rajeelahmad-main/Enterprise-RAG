# Recruitment and Employee Referral Policy
Document ID: HR-019 | Owner: Talent Acquisition

Hiring decisions should be fair, consistent, documented, and based on job-related criteria. Hiring managers define role requirements with Talent Acquisition, review candidates against those requirements, and avoid questions unrelated to the role or prohibited by law.

Employees may refer qualified candidates through the official referral form. A referral does not guarantee an interview or offer. Referral awards, if offered, are governed by the current internal program rules and are payable only after eligibility checks and the required employment period.

Interview notes should be professional, relevant, and stored only in the approved recruitment system. Candidate information must not be shared outside the hiring process.
