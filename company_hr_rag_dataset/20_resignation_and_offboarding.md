# Resignation and Offboarding Procedure
Document ID: HR-020 | Owner: People Operations and IT

Employees who resign should provide written notice to their manager and People Operations according to their employment agreement and applicable law. People Operations confirms the final working date and explains any required handover steps.

Before departure, the manager identifies open work, documentation, client handover, and return of company assets. IT disables access according to the approved departure schedule and preserves records as required. Employees must not retain company confidential information or forward work files to personal accounts.

Finance and People Operations explain final payroll, approved expense claims, benefits end dates, and any required exit documents. Exit feedback is voluntary and should be handled respectfully.
