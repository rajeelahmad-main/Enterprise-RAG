# Grievance and Whistleblowing Procedure
Document ID: ETH-015 | Owner: Compliance and People Operations

Employees may raise concerns about workplace treatment, policy violations, fraud, safety, conflicts of interest, or suspected unlawful conduct. Reports can be made to a manager, People Operations, Compliance, or the confidential reporting channel.

Include dates, people involved, a clear description, and available supporting information. Reporters do not need to prove the allegation before raising a good-faith concern. The company will assess the report, determine an appropriate reviewer, and limit information sharing to those who need it.

Retaliation for a good-faith report is prohibited. Deliberately false reports made with malicious intent may be handled under the code of conduct. Emergency threats to safety should be reported to local emergency services first.
