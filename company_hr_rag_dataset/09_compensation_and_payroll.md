# Compensation and Payroll Guide
Document ID: HR-009 | Owner: Finance and People Operations

Salary is paid monthly on the final business day of each month. If a bank holiday affects processing, Finance will communicate the expected payment date. Employees should verify their bank details through the secure HR portal and never send banking credentials by email or chat.

Payroll questions should be submitted to payroll@northstar.example with the pay period and a description of the issue. Do not include full payment-card details or passwords. Approved changes to salary or recurring allowances must be documented by authorized management and People Operations.

This guide does not set individual salaries and is not a substitute for an employee's signed offer or applicable local requirements.
