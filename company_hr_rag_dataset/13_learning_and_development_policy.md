# Learning and Development Policy
Document ID: HR-013 | Owner: People Operations

Employees may request role-related training, certifications, conferences, or learning subscriptions through their manager. Requests should explain the skill gap, expected business benefit, cost, and time commitment. Approval depends on budget, workload, and role relevance.

Training costing more than 500 fictional currency units requires manager and department-head approval before booking. Employees must not commit the company to a purchase before approval. After a course, the employee should share a brief summary or useful materials with the team when appropriate.

Mandatory security, privacy, safety, and conduct training must be completed by the stated deadline.
