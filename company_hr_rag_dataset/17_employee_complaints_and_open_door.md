# Employee Feedback and Open-Door Procedure
Document ID: HR-017 | Owner: People Operations

Employees are encouraged to raise questions early and respectfully. For routine issues, start with the relevant manager if comfortable doing so. If the issue concerns that manager, is sensitive, or remains unresolved, contact People Operations.

When submitting a concern, state what happened, when it happened, the impact, and the outcome you are seeking. People Operations will acknowledge the concern within three business days and advise on next steps. Complex matters may take longer, and updates will be provided when practical.

This procedure does not replace emergency, security incident, harassment, or whistleblowing channels, which should be used when relevant.
