# New Employee Onboarding Checklist
Document ID: HR-007 | Owner: People Operations

## Before the first day
The hiring manager confirms the start date, role, equipment needs, and onboarding buddy. IT prepares the laptop, work accounts, and required access. People Operations sends the employee the first-week schedule and required forms.

## First day
The employee meets their manager and buddy, reviews the handbook and code of conduct, sets up multifactor authentication, and completes required payroll and emergency-contact forms through approved systems.

## First month
The manager agrees goals for the first 30 days, schedules role-specific training, and holds a check-in at the end of week one and week four. Access not needed for the role should not be granted.
