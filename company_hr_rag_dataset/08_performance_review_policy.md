# Performance Review Policy
Document ID: HR-008 | Owner: People Operations

Formal performance reviews occur twice each year. Employees and managers discuss outcomes against agreed goals, role competencies, collaboration, quality, and development needs. Employees should prepare a short self-review and examples of their work.

Managers should provide specific, balanced feedback and document agreed actions. A review rating does not automatically guarantee a salary increase or promotion; decisions also depend on role scope, budget, business needs, and internal equity.

If performance is below expectations, the manager should explain the gap, provide support, and set measurable improvement goals with a reasonable review period. Employees may add written comments to the review record.
