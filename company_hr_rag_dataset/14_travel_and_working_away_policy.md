# Business Travel Policy
Document ID: FIN-014 | Owner: Finance and Operations

Business travel requires manager approval before tickets or accommodation are booked. Employees should choose reasonable fares and accommodation that balance cost, safety, and business needs. Personal extensions must be clearly separated from business costs.

Keep itemized receipts and submit expenses within 30 days after the trip. Alcohol, personal entertainment, upgrades without approval, and costs for accompanying guests are not reimbursable unless a written exception is authorized in advance.

Before international travel, check security guidance, entry requirements, device-handling requirements, and insurance arrangements with Operations. Do not discuss sensitive company information in public spaces.
