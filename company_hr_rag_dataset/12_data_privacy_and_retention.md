# Data Privacy and Records Retention
Document ID: IT-012 | Owner: Privacy Officer

Collect and use personal data only for a legitimate business purpose and only the minimum amount needed. Access employee, applicant, customer, and supplier records only when authorized for your role. Do not copy personal data to personal devices or share it with unauthorized recipients.

Use approved systems to store records. Follow the retention schedule set by Legal and Compliance; do not independently destroy records subject to an audit, investigation, or legal hold. Requests to correct, access, or delete personal data should be forwarded to the Privacy Officer.

If personal data is sent to the wrong person or exposed unexpectedly, notify IT Security and the Privacy Officer promptly, even if you are unsure whether harm occurred.
