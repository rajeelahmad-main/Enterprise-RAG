# Company Asset and Device Use
Document ID: IT-018 | Owner: IT

Company devices, software accounts, and equipment are provided for authorized business use. Limited personal use is acceptable if lawful, infrequent, secure, and not disruptive. Employees must not disable security controls, install unapproved software, or lend devices to unauthorized people.

Keep equipment physically secure and return it when requested or when employment ends. Report loss, theft, damage, or suspected compromise immediately. IT may monitor company systems to protect security, maintain service, and meet legal obligations, subject to applicable law and published notices.

Do not expect personal files stored on company systems to remain private. Avoid storing personal sensitive data on company equipment.
