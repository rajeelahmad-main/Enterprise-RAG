# Conflict of Interest Policy
Document ID: ETH-016 | Owner: Compliance

Employees should avoid situations where personal, financial, or family interests could improperly influence business decisions or appear to do so. Examples include selecting a supplier owned by a close relative, accepting valuable gifts from a bidder, or using confidential information for personal gain.

Disclose a potential conflict to your manager and Compliance before participating in the related decision. Compliance will assess the facts and may require recusal, additional review, or other safeguards. Do not assume a conflict is acceptable simply because no improper action has occurred.

Modest customary business courtesies may be allowed when infrequent, transparent, and not intended to influence a decision. Cash gifts are never permitted.
